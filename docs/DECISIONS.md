# Decisions and policies

Why the project is the way it is. Read before changing behaviour that looks "odd". Each item says what was decided, why, and what would
justify changing it.

## Product and data

1. **Static site + JSON as the source of truth.** Cheap, auditable, no server to maintain, and the owner is not a programmer. The Cloudflare/D1/R2
   scaffold was abandoned in practice. *Change only if* dynamic features (accounts, comments) are required.
2. **No invented data.** Missing ratings are `null` (rank shown); missing casts are visibly "pending"; a season without `firstEpisode` is skipped, not guessed;
   a Wikipedia page that does not clearly match the actor is rejected. AB/ABC1 numbers come only from TİAK's public tables or from human-reviewed Instagram
   posts, labelled with their source, never overriding TİAK.
3. **Primary sources win.** TİAK and broadcasters are primary; Dizilah is discovery/cross-check (calendar, Star TV casts where the broadcaster publishes none).
4. **Continuous episode numbers** as networks print them ("66. Bölüm"), never per season; the calendar's in-season numbers are converted via `firstEpisode`.
5. **Persian-first.** UI, summaries and news are Persian; Turkish titles stay as a secondary LTR line. Digits and dates use Persian/Jalali `Intl` formats (Tehran time).
6. **Old name kept internally** (`dizimeter.js`, `window.DiziMeter`, `DIZIMETER_*` secrets) — renaming is pure risk.
7. **Expansion order (agreed with the owner):** finish 2026 first, then go year by year backwards (2010–2026 ≈ 1000–1300 titles, 60–90/year), then streaming
   platforms. Build the year/platform/status structure and a "no ratings" label before adding old series (their ratings do not exist in TİAK's window).

## Copyright and privacy

8. **Never copy full third-party articles, subtitles, images or screenshots into the repo.** The site stores titles, links, short own-words Persian text, factual
   metadata; images are **hotlinked** from the source (broadcaster or outlet) with attribution, thumbnails use `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"`.
9. **News text is explanatory, not translated.** The model writes its own 2–4 paragraph explainer from headline + snippet + article opening; the opening is model
   input only and is never stored or displayed.
10. **Sensitive topics are excluded even though the owner said "everything with a source link".** Health, crime/abuse allegations, sexual matters, minors, private
    tragedies are dropped by keyword and by the model. Reason: defamation/privacy risk for real people. Rumours are allowed but labelled «شایعه / ادعا» with a warning.
    Court/lawsuit stories are a grey zone (currently allowed; see ROADMAP).
11. **Wikipedia content is used with credit and licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)** shown on the actor page; machine translation is flagged. **Grokipedia is not used** (licence/API unclear).
12. **Network logos** come from Wikimedia Commons where the licence allows (listed in `automation/network-logos/README.md`), otherwise a generated typographic badge.
    **News outlet "logos"** are coloured wordmark badges; real logos may be dropped in `images/outlets/` by the owner. Broadcaster/outlet trademarks stay with their owners.
13. **Nothing is auto-posted to social networks.** The social publication room prepares cards; a human posts. (A Telegram auto-post is planned, see ROADMAP.)

## News design

14. **One story, many sources.** Duplicate reports are merged (title similarity + shared entities within 72 h). The story keeps the earliest article's id so links never break. Each
    outlet remains visible via its badge, with a link to its original article — this is the user's requirement (logos, not just text links).
15. **Cross-source comparison ("Jan-Kalam style")**: what all outlets agree on / where they differ / what is unconfirmed, generated by the same Gemini call as the story.
16. **Persian only in public.** A story without `titleFa` is invisible on the site; there is no "Turkish fallback" any more.
17. **Gemini as in Jan-Kalam**, API key in the `AI_API_KEY` secret (comma-separated keys rotate). Model names are discovered at run time because they retire without notice.
18. **Failure never blocks the site.** Collect/bios steps are `continue-on-error`; persistence is best-effort with 3 retries; diagnostics go to the run summary.
19. **Retention:** general entertainment 14 days, other stories 45 days, stories with their own page 120 days, cap 800 — permalinks of the last four months should keep working.

## Operations

20. **The owner publishes through an AI assistant and the GitHub web UI**, so the repository must stay editable that way (no build step required locally, small self-contained files,
    generated files regenerated by CI).
21. **Secrets are entered only by the owner.** Assistants must not type keys, passwords or tokens anywhere and must not print them in logs or chat.
22. **Runs queue, never cancel** (`concurrency`): a run may be deploying or committing data.
23. **Bot commits use `[skip ci]`** so data persistence does not trigger another build; the deploy that produced the data has already published it.
24. **Quality gates are strict for curated shows** (`audit-content.mjs --require …`) and for all casts (`audit-cast.mjs`); a red build is preferable to publishing malformed editorial data.
25. **Cache-busting by hand** (`?v=`) because GitHub Pages cannot set headers.

## Explicitly not done

- No ratings scraped from social media engagement; no AB/ABC1 numbers invented.
- No embedded third-party video players that autoplay: YouTube is click-to-load via `youtube-nocookie.com`.
- No user accounts, comments, analytics beyond Search Console.
- No Grokipedia, no article scraping for display, no paywalled sources.
